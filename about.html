About A small studio in Malta Est. on the island

Our Story

Nestart Home was born out of a passion for interior aesthetics in Malta. We curate modern, high-quality poster decorations designed to elevate contemporary homes, offices, and creative spaces without breaking the bank.

We take our cues from the island around us: honey-coloured limestone, wooden gallariji, a luzzu bobbing in Marsaxlokk, the long Gozo sunsets. Then we turn them into clean, contemporary prints that sit as comfortably in a Sliema apartment as in a converted farmhouse or a busy Valletta office.

How we work

Curated slowly, delivered quickly.

01

Curated in Malta

Each design is drawn or commissioned around one idea: a place, a shape or a phrase that feels like home. Only a handful make the collection.

02

Printed to last

200 gsm responsibly sourced matte paper and water-based inks give soft, glare-free colour in standard A4, A3 and 50×70 cm sizes.

03

Packed by hand

Every order is checked, rolled or flat-packed in recycled card, and dispatched from Malta within 1–3 working days. Gozo included.
